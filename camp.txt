### **Aims & Objectives**

1. To empower youth in different subjects for making worthy Human beings.
2. To promote national integration, unity, and peace through youth exchange and collaborative activities.
3. To develop leadership and responsibility among youth.
4. To support Science research and innovation in youth-related issues and policies.
5. To arouse consciousness about the Cultural Heritage of our Country.
6. To lend a helping hand to poor & meritorious students and the aged, sick and helpless people.
7. To engage youth in social service and community development to foster a spirit of volunteerism.
8. To foster the spirit of Literacy, Scientific and Technical Education.
9. To promote youth affairs related Scientific and Cultural projects of the Central and State Government.
10. To popularize the idea of democracy among the masses and also to generate the spirit of Nationalism.
11. To arrange for seminars, lectures, meetings, exhibitions etc. at National Level, relating to Science and Culture and publish papers, bulletins, magazines etc.
12. To train the youths in Self-development Schemes, Youth & National Integration.
13. To strengthen youth participation in democratic and developmental processes.
14. To provide training and skill development to enhance employability and entrepreneurship.
15. To engage youth in social service and community development to foster a spirit of volunteerism.

### **Mission Vision Approach**

Since 2014, the renowned social, voluntary and philanthropic society “Institute of Youth Affairs” was founded, it celebrates its journey with various annual commitments by yearly organized such National Integration Camp, National Seminars, National Science Awareness Campaign, and workshops to encourage the young minds in Science, to exchange mutually our cultural ties between students & youth of different corner of India, to make an eternal bond between students of all over India with the aim of implementing people-centered and community-oriented development programs with permanent impact on their life.

Our motto is to draw inspiration among students & youth from the vibrant colours of India’s national flag saffron, white and green, and the blue chakra that reflects growth amid challenges. We generously organize this National Integration and Youth Leadership Camp since the organisation’s inception and it has been 11 years of oath to convey a powerful message among the society by delivering our selfless care and service to bring the most remarkable change of this era. We have had already successfully arranged this National Integration Camp in different states of India. In India, we have covered 11 states are witnesses in this National Integration and Youth Leadership Camp viz Karnataka, Rajasthan, Goa, Meghalaya, Gujarat, Assam, Tamil Nadu, Tripura, Andaman & Nicobar Island, Arunachal Pradesh and previous year in Sikkim.

In this National Integration Camp, mainly the participants are youth and students of various classes and the different age group from 14 to 22, are the future-generation of India, and here we also consider them as the pillar of new India. Throughout the camp period, we offer them efficient trainings on different courses and also happily arrange their lunch & refreshments* during the eight days of the National Integration and Youth leadership Camp. Training subjects have been carefully chosen such as Yoga, Aerobics, Drill, Self-Defence, Parade, Career Guidance, Technical Education, Music & Dance and Audio-Drama by experts, high dignitaries, vice-chancellors, professors, trainers and so on as well as on self-defense, first-aid training for their self-development and self- help, self-reliant and a discipline life. In this backdrop we mainly choose those subjects for training which can be beneficial for them in a long-run and a way to learn Indian brotherhood culture in this session after the camp period and all over their life. Overall, in this training period, the students & youth all stands up together and stay here for 8 days in the camp which made them disciplined, they can understand the value of time, mutual cooperation and friendliness. With the time participated students found that there are multi-cultural, multi-lingual, multi-racial people which help them to stay unite and develop a sense of integrity, which is positively help them to grow in their future endeavors.

Dr. Himanta Biswa Sarma, Hon’ble Chief Minister, Government of Assam; Dr. Sukanta Majumdar, Hon’ble Minister of State for Education & Development of North Eastern Region, Govt. of India, Shri Parimal Suklabaidya, Hon’ble Member of Parliament, Lok Sabha, Shri Kanad Purkayastha, Hon’ble Member of Parliament, Lok Sabha Shri Kaushik Rai, Hon’ble Minister, Department of Food, Public, Distribution & Consumer Affairs, Mines & Minerals, Barak Valley Development, Government of Assam & other distinguished VVIPs will be present at this National Integration Camp on different date & time during 24th to 31st December 2025. It will make this event more superior. Eminent persons and high dignitaries of North-East India will also be present at this auspicious ceremony. We heartily invite all of you to kindly be present in the above National programme.

Our “National Integration Camp” will be held at the different venues of the Assam University, Silchar on the theme of “Empowering Youth For Godeen India” on and from 24th to 31st December 2025. Moreover, this year we expect more than 500 participants from the North-east States including, Sikkim, Arunachal Pradesh, Assam, Tripura, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram and Nagaland at this camp. This year we will be holding various National Seminars by eminent scientists and professors and also a career guidance and counsellors by the Indian Defence Sector. These innumerable benefits at National Integration Camp can only be realized when acquire by the students of North-east in Itanagar by taking part at this National Integration Camp which allows them to access truly an inclusive training at free of cost.

To execute our noble aim, we cordially welcome all the students and youths to participate in this camp. With the kind help, support and co-operation from Ministry of Home affairs, Ministry of Defense, Ministry of Culture, North-Eastern Council, other PSUs of Government of India and State Government and all the people of India mainly the North-East, we will be able to accomplish this dream for the upliftment of the people and the future India.
