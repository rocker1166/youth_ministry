**ABOUT SCIENCE EXHIBITION & 12TH NATIONAL INTEGRATION CAMP**

Since 2014, the renowned social, voluntary and philanthropic society "Institute of Youth Affairs" was founded, it celebrates its journey with various annual commitments by yearly organized such National Integration Camp, National Seminars, National Science Awareness Campaign, Science Exhibition and workshops to encourage the young minds in Science, to exchange mutually on cultural ties between students & youth of different corner of India, to make an eternal bond between students of all over India with the aim of implementing people-centered and community-oriented development programs with permanent impact on their life.

Our motto is to draw inspiration among students & youth from the vibrant colours of India's national flag saffron, white and green, and the blue chakra that reflects growth amid challenges. We generously organize this National Integration and Youth Leadership Camp since the organisation's inception and it has been 11 years of oath to convey a powerful message among the society by delivering our selfless care and service to bring the most remarkable change of this era. We have had already successfully arranged this National Integration Camp in different states of India. In India, we have covered 11 states are witness in this National Integration and Youth Leadership Camp viz Karnataka, Rajasthan, Goa, Meghalaya, Gujarat, Assam, Tamil Nādu, Tripura, Andaman & Nicobar Island, Arunachal Pradesh and previous year in Sikkim.

This year we specially have thought about the youth development by their interest in science & its innovations through a Science Exhibition. A Science Exhibition will provide a platform for them to encourage STEM Careers, to connect with Scientific Communication, to boost Confidence and Self-Esteem, to bridge Theory and Practice, to enhance Communication and Presentation Abilities, to foster Creativity and Innovation, to promote innovation, and to develop crucial skills like critical thinking, creativity, and public speaking and also to increase the interest in science research.

So, a Science Exhibition for youth is very important. We are going to organize a SCIENCE EXHIBITION on the occasion of 12TH NATIONAL INTEGRATION CAMP on & from 28th to 30th December 2025 during 11 a.m. to 7 p.m. at the Assam University, Silchar in cooperation with the Assam University & Government of Assam for the benefits of youth of College & University of North East States (Assam, Tripura, Manipur, Meghalaya, Mizoram, Nagaland, Arunachal Pradesh & Sikkim). ## **ABOUT THEME : EMPOWERING YOUTH THROUGH SCIENCE & TECHNOLOGY TO ACHIEVE VIKSIT & SWARNIM BHARAT**

The future of any nation lies in the hands of its youth, and their empowerment is the key to creating a Golden Nation. Science has always been the foundation of progress, and empowering youth in science is essential to building a Golden India. The upcoming Science Exhibition, to be held at Assam University, Silchar from 28th to 30th December 2025, aims to spark curiosity, creativity, and innovation among young minds, inspiring them to find scientific solutions to the challenges of the future.

The theme, “Empowering Youth Through Science & Technology To Achieve Viksit & Swarnim Bharat” focuses on nurturing scientific temper, research-oriented thinking, and problem-solving skills. By encouraging students to present their models, experiments, and innovative ideas, this exhibition provides them with a platform to explore new technologies, share knowledge, and collaborate on sustainable solutions for society.

When youth are equipped with scientific knowledge and critical thinking, they become agents of transformation driving breakthroughs in healthcare, agriculture, energy, space research, and environmental conservation. This empowerment will pave the way for a technologically advanced, inclusive, and self-reliant India.

The Science Exhibition offers numerous benefits for the participating departments. It provides a prestigious platform to showcase their research, innovations, and achievements, giving both demonstrators and students valuable exposure. Departments gain opportunities to collaborate with other universities, research institutions, and industries. Students benefit from hands-on experience, confidence-building. Recognition through certificates and appreciation further adds to the academic profile of the departments, while contributing to the larger goal of nation-building by inspiring young minds to work on sustainable solutions for a Golden India.

This Science Exhibition, organized in cooperation with the Government of Assam and Assam University, Silchar, is a celebration of youth potential and a step towards building an inclusive and visionary future for the nation. The Science Exhibition celebrates the limitless potential of young innovators and their vital role in creating a prosperous and progressive nation.
